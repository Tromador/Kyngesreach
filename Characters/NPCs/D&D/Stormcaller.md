## Features and Traits

### Class and Subclass Features

- **Primal Order — Magician.** One additional Druid cantrip; add Wisdom modifier to Intelligence (Arcana and Nature) checks.
- **Druidic.** Knows Druidic and always has *Speak with Animals* prepared.
- **Wild Shape — 3 uses.** Bonus Action; duration up to 8 hours. Regain one use on a Short Rest and all uses on a Long Rest. Each transformation grants 16 Temporary Hit Points.
- **Known Wild Shape Forms:** Giant Octopus, Reef Shark, Giant Bat, Giant Spider, Dire Wolf, Brown Bear, Riding Horse, Rat.
- **Wild Companion.** Expend a spell slot or Wild Shape use to cast *Find Familiar* without Material components; familiar is Fey and lasts until the next Long Rest.
- **Wild Resurgence.** Exchange a spell slot for a Wild Shape use if none remain; once per Long Rest, exchange a Wild Shape use for a 1st-level spell slot.
- **Potent Spellcasting.** Add Wisdom modifier to Druid-cantrip damage. At level 15, a Druid cantrip with a spell range of at least 10 feet gains 300 feet of range.
- **Wrath of the Sea.** Bonus Action and one Wild Shape use creates a 10-foot Emanation for 10 minutes. On manifestation, and as a Bonus Action subsequently, one visible creature within it makes a DC 18 Constitution save; on failure, it takes 5d6 Cold damage and a Large or smaller target can be pushed up to 15 feet.
- **Aquatic Affinity.** Swim Speed equals normal Speed.
- **Stormborn.** While Wrath of the Sea is active, Fly Speed equals normal Speed and gain Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Thunder.
- **Oceanic Gift.** Wrath of the Sea can instead be manifested around a willing creature within 60 feet. Spending two Wild Shape uses manifests it around both that creature and the Druid.

### Species Traits

- **Human — Skillful.** Deception proficiency.
- **Human — Versatile.** Alert Origin feat.
- **Resourceful.** Gain Heroic Inspiration after finishing a Long Rest.

### Feats and Origin Features

- **Healer — Hermit.** A Healer's Kit can be used to let an adjacent creature spend a Hit Die for healing; healing dice that roll 1 can be rerolled.
- **Alert — Human.** Add Proficiency Bonus to Initiative; may exchange Initiative with a willing ally immediately after rolling.
- **War Caster — Level 4.** +1 Wisdom; Advantage on Constitution saves to maintain Concentration; may cast a suitable single-target spell instead of an Opportunity Attack; somatic components remain usable with hands occupied.
- **Ability Score Improvement — Level 8.** +2 Wisdom.
- **Resilient (Constitution) — Level 12.** +1 Constitution and Constitution saving-throw proficiency.
- **Skill Expert — Level 16.** +1 Constitution; Survival proficiency; Expertise in Perception.

## Character Notes

- **Appearance:** Usually presents as a weathered island healer, navigator, or reclusive hermit rather than an obvious cult authority.
- **Background / Personality:** To outsiders, the Deep Voice is the sort of island wise person sailors seek before a dangerous passage and afterwards insist they never consulted. Within the Stormcallers, they interpret unusual tides, currents, animal behaviour, and storms as signs of imbalance. Reports from Hidden Hands reach them when explorers, merchants, or scholars begin asking the wrong questions.
- **Tactics / Encounter Role:** The Deep Voice decides whether an intrusion deserves watching, a warning, deliberate misdirection, or removal. Their preference is to make the archipelago itself appear responsible: ruined charts, unnatural fog, impossible winds, closed waters, and only then catastrophic weather. They are a senior ritual authority rather than a front-line brawler, but are fully capable of personally enforcing the cult's judgement.
- **Doctrine:** The Sea Remembers. Balance Above All. The Storm Calls the Unworthy.
- **The Drowned:** The character may possess cult authority over the Drowned through Stormcaller rites and hierarchy, but no nonstandard Druid ability to create or command undead is assumed here.
